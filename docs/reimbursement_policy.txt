ABC TECHNOLOGIES - EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1. INTERNET AND REMOTE WORK ALLOWANCE
Employees working under the hybrid or full-remote program are eligible for a monthly internet allowance of up to $75 USD. Receipts must be uploaded to the Finance Expense Portal by the 25th of each month.

2. TRAVEL EXPENSES
Official business travel must be pre-approved by the department head. Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy Class. Hotel accommodations are capped at $200 USD per night for standard cities and $300 USD per night for high-cost tier-1 cities.

3. MEALS AND INCIDENTALS
A daily per-diem allowance of $60 USD applies for domestic business travel. For team lunches and business entertainment, expenses up to $40 USD per person are reimbursable with prior manager approval and detailed itemized receipts.

4. REIMBURSEMENT PROCESSING TIME
All submitted expense reports with valid receipts are processed by Finance within 10 business days following approval. Approved reimbursements are credited directly to the employee's designated payroll bank account.
