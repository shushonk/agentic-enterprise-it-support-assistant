ABC TECHNOLOGIES - CORPORATE VPN GUIDE AND POLICY (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1. MANDATORY VPN USAGE
ABC Technologies employees must connect to the corporate GlobalProtect VPN whenever accessing internal corporate systems, development databases, staging servers, or internal portals from outside the corporate office network.

2. VPN GATEWAY ENDPOINTS
- Americas Region: vpn-us.abctechnologies.com
- EMEA Region: vpn-eu.abctechnologies.com
- APAC Region: vpn-ap.abctechnologies.com

3. CONNECTING TO VPN
1. Launch the Palo Alto GlobalProtect VPN Client on your ABC corporate laptop.
2. Select your regional gateway endpoint from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your ABC corporate Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials (email and password).
4. Complete Okta Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on your registered mobile device.
5. Once connected, the status icon in your system tray will display a blue checkmark ("Connected").

4. TROUBLESHOOTING VPN ISSUES
- If connection fails with "Authentication Timeout", verify your internet connection and re-try MFA.
- If DNS resolution fails, open Command Prompt and run `ipconfig /flushdns`.
- For persistent connection drops, restart the GlobalProtect service via Services Manager or reboot your laptop.
- If you are still unable to connect, create an IT Support Ticket via the IT Service Portal or contact the IT Helpdesk at support@abctechnologies.com.
