ABC TECHNOLOGIES - HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1. WORKING HOURS AND HYBRID WORK
ABC Technologies operates on a 40-hour work week standard (Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time). All full-time employees are eligible for the Hybrid Work Model, which permits working from home up to 2 days per week with prior manager authorization.

2. CODE OF CONDUCT AND ETHICS
Employees must maintain professional conduct at all times. ABC Technologies strictly prohibits discrimination, harassment, or retaliation of any kind based on race, gender, religion, national origin, age, disability, or sexual orientation. Violations may result in formal disciplinary action up to immediate termination.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
All employee, customer, and proprietary project data processed at ABC Technologies is classified under strict confidentiality guidelines. Employees must not transfer company data to unauthorized personal devices or public cloud services.

4. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION AND PROMOTIONS
Performance reviews take place bi-annually in June and December. Promotions and merit increments are evaluated based on performance review outcomes, manager recommendations, and business requirements.
