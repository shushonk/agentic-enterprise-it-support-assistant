ABC TECHNOLOGIES - LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1. ANNUAL LEAVE ALLOCATION
Full-time employees at ABC Technologies accrue 20 paid leave days per calendar year. Leave accrual begins on the first day of employment and is calculated on a monthly pro-rata basis.

2. SICK AND MEDICAL LEAVE
Employees are allocated 10 paid sick leave days per year. For medical absences exceeding 3 consecutive working days, a signed medical certificate from a registered physician must be submitted to HR via the HR Portal within 48 hours of returning to work.

3. CASUAL AND PERSONAL LEAVE
Employees receive 5 casual leave days per year for personal matters. Casual leave requests should be submitted at least 24 hours in advance through the HR Portal and require supervisor approval.

4. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
ABC Technologies provides 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for female employees and 4 weeks of paid paternity leave for primary/secondary caregivers. Requests should be submitted 30 days prior to the expected commencement date.

5. HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
ABC Technologies observes 10 mandatory public holidays annually, published at the beginning of each calendar year by the HR Operations department.
