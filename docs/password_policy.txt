ABC TECHNOLOGIES - CORPORATE PASSWORD & IDENTITY POLICY (DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
All employee SSO passwords at ABC Technologies must satisfy the following security criteria:
- Minimum length: 14 characters.
- Must include at least 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 numerical digit, and 1 special character (!@#$%^&*).
- Cannot contain the user's first name, last name, or login ID.
- Cannot match any of the previous 10 passwords used.

2. EXPIRY AND EXPIRATION
Corporate SSO passwords expire every 90 days. Users will receive automated email reminders 14 days, 7 days, and 1 day prior to expiration.

3. PASSWORD RESET PROCEDURE
If you forget your password or get locked out:
1. Initiate a Self-Service Password Reset via the IT Support Assistant or identity portal.
2. Verify identity using a One-Time Passcode (OTP) sent to your registered contact.
3. Upon entering the correct OTP, you will be prompted to choose a new password meeting policy standards.
4. If invalid OTP is entered 3 times consecutively, the account is temporarily locked for security, and an IT support incident is automatically dispatched to ServiceNow.

4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
MFA via Okta Verify Push or Hardware Security Key (YubiKey) is mandatory for all access logins. SMS authentication is prohibited for privileged access accounts.
